# Snooze Tones Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This policy explains what information the Snooze Tones app for Android ("the app") uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The app is published by **[[COMPANY_NAME]]** (CNPJ [[CNPJ]]), [[ADDRESS]], Brazil ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the information described here. For any privacy question or request, write to **[[CONTACT_EMAIL]]**.

## The short version

- The app has **no account and no server of its own**. Your Favorites, theme, language and other settings stay on your phone.
- The app shows **ads from Google AdMob**, unless you buy Remove Ads.
- The app sends **usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase**, so we can see which sounds are used and fix problems.
- **Purchases** go through Google Play, and RevenueCat checks them. We never see your payment details.
- In the **EEA, the UK and Switzerland**, the app asks for your consent before any personalized ads or statistics, and you can change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.

## Information that stays on your phone

The app stores your Favorites, theme, language, sleep timer choices and similar settings **only on your device**. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage, and they are not backed up or synced by us.

## Information processed by third-party services

The app uses the services below. Each provider receives the information directly from the app and handles it under its own privacy policy as well as this one.

### Ads: Google AdMob

Unless you own Remove Ads, the app shows a banner on the Home and Category screens and, from time to time, a full-screen ad when you start a sound. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob collects:

- your device's **advertising ID**,
- your **IP address** and the approximate location derived from it,
- **device and app information** (device model, Android version, language, app version),
- **ad interactions** (which ads were shown and tapped).

Where allowed, Google uses this to show **personalized ads** based on your interests. Where you decline personalized ads (see "Your choices"), Google shows **non-personalized ads**, which still use some of this information for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops loading ads entirely.

Google's policy: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Usage statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

To learn which content people use, the app records a small, fixed set of events:

- a sound started, added to a mix, or removed from it (with the sound's catalog name, such as "light rain"),
- a preset started (with the preset's catalog name),
- a sleep timer set or completed (with its length in minutes),
- Remove Ads purchased or restored.

These events contain no names, email addresses, or anything you type. Firebase also collects some information automatically: an **app instance ID**, the **advertising ID**, **device and app information** (model, Android version, language, app version), **approximate location** (country or region, derived from your IP address), and when and how long the app is used.

We keep this data for [[ANALYTICS_RETENTION]], and then Google deletes it. Reports we view are aggregated.

### Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the technical details of the error, the device model, Android version, app version, the time, and a **Crashlytics installation ID**. We use these only to find and fix bugs. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Crash reports follow the same switch as usage statistics: when statistics are off, crash reports are off too.

Google's policies: [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Purchases: Google Play and RevenueCat

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through **Google Play**. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

To check that the purchase is valid and restore it on a new phone, the app uses **RevenueCat**. When the app starts, it checks with RevenueCat whether you own Remove Ads. RevenueCat receives a **random anonymous user ID** created by the app, your **purchase receipt** (product, price, currency, store country, and transaction details), and basic **device and app information**. It is not linked to your name or email.

RevenueCat's policy: [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Permissions

The app asks for only these Android permissions:

- **Notifications**: to show playback controls while a sound is playing, so you can pause or stop it from the notification or lock screen.
- **Foreground service (media playback)**: so sounds keep playing with the screen off.

The app does not access your location (beyond the approximate region the services above derive from your IP address), contacts, camera, microphone, files, or photos.

## Your choices

**If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland**, the app asks for your consent the first time you open it, using Google's consent tool, before it loads any ad or sends any statistics. You can:

- accept or decline personalized ads,
- accept or decline usage statistics (which also controls crash reports),
- change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.

The app works fully whether you accept or decline.

**Everywhere else**, personalized ads, usage statistics and crash reports are on by default, based on our legitimate interest (see "Why we use this information"). You can still:

- **delete or reset your advertising ID** in your phone's settings (on most phones, **Settings → Privacy → Ads** or **Settings → Google → Ads**). Once it is deleted, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads,
- **buy Remove Ads**, which stops all ads from loading,
- **uninstall the app**, which stops all collection and deletes everything stored on your phone.

You can also ask us to stop processing your information, as described in "Your rights".

## Why we use this information (legal bases)

- **Showing ads** keeps the app free. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalized ads rely on your **consent**. Elsewhere they rely on our **legitimate interest** in funding the app, and you can opt out at any time as described in "Your choices".
- **Usage statistics and crash reports** let us improve content and fix bugs. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland they rely on your **consent**. Elsewhere they rely on our **legitimate interest** in keeping the app working and useful. They use only the few fields listed above, are not linked to your name, and are not used to track you across other apps.
- **Purchase checks** are needed to **deliver what you bought** (performance of a contract).

For users in Brazil, the corresponding bases of the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) apply: consent (art. 7, I), legitimate interest (art. 7, IX) and performance of a contract (art. 7, V). Where we rely on legitimate interest, we process only the information needed for the purpose and you can object at any time (art. 10 and art. 18, § 2).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the LGPD in Brazil, the GDPR in the EEA and UK, and US state privacy laws), you may have the right to:

- know what information is processed and get a copy of it,
- correct or delete it,
- object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent at any time,
- ask that it be transferred to another service,
- complain to a data protection authority. In Brazil this is the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

To make a request, email **[[CONTACT_EMAIL]]**. This address is our privacy contact channel. As a small processing agent under ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD 2/2022, we have not appointed a data protection officer (encarregado). We reply within 15 days. Because the app has no accounts, the statistics and purchase records we can access are not linked to your name. To help us find your data, we may ask for details such as the date of your purchase or your Google Play order number.

## Security

Information sent by the app to the services above is encrypted in transit. We limit access to the Firebase, AdMob and RevenueCat consoles to the people who need it.

Snooze Tones is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has used the app and information about them was collected, contact us and we will delete what we can.
